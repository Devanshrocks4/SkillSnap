# 🎯 Problem Statement

The problem SkillSnap solves.

---

## The Problem

Recruitment is broken in 2026.

### For recruiters

- **Resume overload**: a single job posting can attract 500+ applications. Manually screening them takes days.
- **Inconsistent evaluation**: different recruiters score the same candidate differently.
- **Slow time-to-hire**: top candidates accept other offers while you're still screening.
- **No actionable insights**: spreadsheets don't tell you why candidate A is better than B.

### For candidates

- **Black-box rejections**: candidates never know why they were rejected.
- **Slow feedback loops**: weeks pass with no update.
- **Poor job matching**: generic job boards recommend irrelevant roles.
- **Clunky onboarding**: once hired, paperwork is fragmented.

### For hiring orgs

- **No hiring intelligence**: leadership can't see funnel health, time-to-hire, or offer acceptance trends.
- **Compliance risk**: no audit trail for hiring decisions.
- **Tool sprawl**: ATS + email + spreadsheets + video calls = chaos.

---

## Why Now?

Three forces converge to make this solvable today:

1. **LLMs are good enough** — Gemini can read a resume and produce a recruiter-quality summary in seconds.
2. **Serverless is cheap** — Firebase + Cloud Functions make it possible to ship a global product with zero ops.
3. **Candidates expect better** — after ChatGPT, every product feels slow and dumb.

---

## Our Solution

SkillSnap is an **AI-native recruitment platform** that:

- **Screens resumes in seconds** with Gemini
- **Ranks candidates with explainable scores** — not black-box magic
- **Recommends jobs to candidates** using skill-overlap scoring
- **Evaluates interviews** from transcripts
- **Tracks onboarding** with document verification
- **Visualizes hiring health** with real-time analytics

All on a **premium, dark-mode-first UI** that feels like a funded AI startup — not an enterprise ATS from 2009.
